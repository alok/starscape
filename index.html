    How to move

    • Drag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag or two fingers to pan.
    • At the origin: drag the sky to move every star by a Möbius map. Right-drag or two fingers to look around. W A S D lean your eye off the origin.
    • Tap a star to read its polynomial. Shift-tap a second star for the geodesic through both.
